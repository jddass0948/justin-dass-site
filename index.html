About

I've spent 20+ years growing revenue in enterprise software, SaaS, ERP and AI, at companies ranging from early-stage to Autodesk to private equity portfolios.

Today I'm CRO and Global Vice President, Revenue at ASTERRA. We sell AI-powered, satellite-based infrastructure intelligence to utilities, municipalities and enterprise infrastructure organizations, and I lead worldwide sales across 65+ countries in the Americas, EMEA and APAC.
